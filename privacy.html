Privacy
Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Hotmess Studios (Hot Mess Studios), a creative studio based in Stockholm, Sweden, also working in Paris. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. For anything privacy-related, email contact@hotmess.se.

The short version

  • There are no forms, accounts or newsletter sign-ups on this site.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your data for marketing.
  • We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which works without cookies.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to answer you and handle your booking.

Emails and booking enquiries

When you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it to reply, give quotes and plan shoots, studio rentals or other work with you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and, where a booking follows, taking steps towards and performing a contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b)).

We keep enquiry emails for as long as the conversation or project is active and for up to 24 months after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. If an enquiry turns into paid work, related invoices and records are kept for seven years as required by the Swedish Bookkeeping Act (bokföringslagen).

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. To deliver pages and keep the site secure, Vercel processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Vercel acts as our processor. Data may be processed outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they view. It does not use cookies and does not follow you across other websites. Visitors are counted using a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours, so we can't identify you. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used.

Fonts

Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address. Google states that it does not use this data to build profiles. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Cookies and local storage

We don't set any cookies. When you click Accept or Decline in the cookie notice, we save that choice in your browser's local storage (key hm-cookie-consent) so we don't ask again. You can change it any time with the Cookie Settings link in the footer, or clear it in your browser settings.

Instagram and other links

The photos on this site are hosted by us, so no Instagram content is embedded. When you follow a link to Instagram or another site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to our use of it, and receive it in a portable format. Email contact@hotmess.se and we will reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY) at imy.se.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
